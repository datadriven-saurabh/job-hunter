# Comprehensive Guide: LinkedIn Referral Requests to New Connections

Requesting a referral from a new LinkedIn connection requires a balance of professional courtesy, extreme brevity, and zero-friction execution.

---

## 1. Technical Specifications & Constraints

| Parameter | Guideline / Constraint |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Word Count Limit** | 75 – 125 words (Max 3 short paragraphs) |
| **Connection Note Limit** | 300 characters (if sending within the initial invitation) |
| **Timing** | Send immediately after connection acceptance or via InMail |
| **File / Link Standard** | Public Google Drive/Dropbox resume link or Portfolio URL |
| **Required Data Points** | Exact Job Title, Job ID / Req Number, 1–2 Core Skills |

---

## 3. Ready-to-Use Message Templates

### Template 1: Post-Connection Referral Message (Standard)
* **Target Length:** ~100 words
* **Best For:** Messages sent after a user accepts your connection request.

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting! I noticed an opening for the [Job Title] role (Job ID: #[XXXXX]) at [Company Name], and given your work with the [Team/Department Name] team, I wanted to reach out.

I have [X] years of experience in [2 key skills/technologies], and my background aligns closely with the role's requirements. 

If you feel comfortable submitting a referral, I would be immensely grateful. Here is a link to my resume for quick review: [Resume Link]. 

Either way, thank you for your time and consideration!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

---

### Template 2: Connection Request Note (300 Character Limit)
* **Target Length:** <300 characters
* **Best For:** Sending directly inside the initial LinkedIn invite note.

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting! I'm applying for the [Job Title] position (ID: #[XXXXX]) at [Company]. With my background in [Key Skill], I’d love to contribute to the team. If open to sharing an internal referral, I’d be super grateful! My resume: [Shortened Link]. Best, [Your Name].
```

---

### Template 3: Mutual Connection / Shared Group Context
* **Target Length:** ~110 words
* **Best For:** Reaching out when you share a mutual group, alma mater, or mutual contact.

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope you're having a great week! I saw that we both share a background in [University/Group/Domain]. 

I am currently applying for the [Job Title] role (Job ID: #[XXXXX]) at [Company Name]. Having built scalable projects in [Skill A] and [Skill B], I believe I'd be a strong technical fit for the team.

If you are open to referring me internally, I’d deeply appreciate it. I’ve made my resume accessible here: [Resume Link].

Thanks again for your time!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## 4. Execution Rules & Best Practices

- [x] **Always Include the Job ID:** Enterprise companies have multiple similar postings. Eliminate searching hassle for the referrer.
- [x] **Provide a Frictionless Resume Link:** Use a clean, public link (Google Drive, Dropbox, or Portfolio) so they don't have to message back asking for a PDF.
- [x] **Avoid "Brain Picking" Calls:** If your immediate goal is a referral, do not ask for a 30-minute introductory call. Be direct and respect their time.
- [x] **Provide a Clear Opt-Out:** Phrases like *"If you feel comfortable..."* reduce pressure and make connections more willing to respond.
- [x] **Follow-Up Rule:** Send at most **one** follow-up message after 4–5 business days. If there is no response, move on.
