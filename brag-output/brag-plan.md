# Brag Plan: Job Hunter

## What is this app?
Job Hunter brings job discovery, evidence-based matching, and reviewed application drafts into one private workspace.

## The angle
A job search that shows its work. Follow one fictional example opening from search to a clear fit explanation and a prepared application kit. This is a product demonstration, not a claim that any example company is hiring.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
An empty job-search screen resolves into the line “Your search, with receipts.” Then the actual product name appears.

## Key moments
- The Discover jobs interface receives a “Data Analyst” search and returns a sample opportunity.
- The fit panel shows matched skills and one visible gap, so the score has evidence.
- Application Studio turns the selected opening into an ATS resume and outreach drafts marked ready for review.

## Outro / punchline
“Focus your search. Make your move.” This is the README tagline. End on the Job Hunter mark.

## User flow worth showing
Enter a role in Discover jobs → inspect a sample match and its evidence → open Application Studio and review prepared drafts.

## Nine-question planning rubric
1. App: a private job discovery and application preparation workspace.
2. Strongest claim: “Focus your search. Make your move.”
3. Visual hook: the navy journey card, violet-to-blue action gradient, and white opportunity cards.
4. Actual UI: Discover jobs search, opportunity fit details, and Application Studio assets.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 21 seconds.
6. Tone: app-store, with a focused product-film direction and restrained motion.
7. Audio: clean mid-tempo bed plus soft UI/card accents.
8. Share caption: “Built Job Hunter: discover openings, see the evidence behind a match, and prepare a reviewed application in one workspace.”
9. Flow: role query → fit evidence → application kit for review.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clear, calm, credible product film
- Interpretation: confident type, quick interface actions, spacious holds, and no invented performance claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: `#f4f7fb`
- Accent: `#5746c8`, graduating to `#387bd9` on primary buttons
- Text: `#14233a`
- Display font: Manrope in the app; Outfit bundled fallback in the video
- Body font: DM Sans in the app; Inter bundled fallback in the video
- Strongest visual element: dark navy journey card framed by the light dashboard.
- Privacy: every company, score, skill detail, and personal field shown in this video is a clearly fictional sample. No live app data is captured.

## Share copy (draft)
Built Job Hunter: discover openings, see the evidence behind a match, and prepare a reviewed application in one workspace.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a steady music bed.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` by Sascha Ende.
- Music treatment: quiet at first, around 0.30 through the UI sequence, then fade under the final mark.
- Music cue guidance: 110 BPM preset; the strong beat near 8.74s supports the fit reveal. The kit-ready message enters at 15.85s so it can hold long enough to read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: extracted music RMS subtly changes the search button shadow; no waveform or pulse on text.
- SFX posture: three or four soft click/card sounds tied to visible actions.
- Audio-coupled moments: query submit, match card reveal, kit ready state.
- Restraint rule: sounds must never overpower the legible product screens.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The hook — 2.5s
A navy field and the actual Job Hunter target mark. “Your search, with receipts.” settles and holds. The product name arrives underneath.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: a clean opening accent over a low bed.
Audio-coupled idea: title reveal.
Music: steady, restrained.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Discover — 5s
Recreate the app’s Discover jobs screen: sidebar, heading “Find your next opportunity.”, role search “Data Analyst”, and the Find opportunities button. A cursor submits the query; a fictional sample result appears. Search text holds long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: yes, typed role then one button click.
Audio intent: soft interaction click.
Audio-coupled idea: query and result card.
Music: bed gains presence.
Transition mood: slide into result → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — See the evidence — 5s
The opportunity detail view shows “Sample opportunity,” a fit explanation, “Matched: SQL · Python,” and “Check: Tableau” as explicitly fictional example content. The card surfaces one insight at a time, then holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes, evidence rows arrive one by one; use music pulse only where readability allows.
Audio intent: card accents, no staccato barrage.
Audio-coupled idea: fit panel reveal near 8.74s.
Music: steady.
Transition mood: select the job → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Prepare, then review — 5.5s
Recreate Application Studio’s two-column workspace. An ATS resume tile and outreach draft tiles appear; a “Ready for review” state confirms the human review step. Do not suggest automatic submission.
Sequential/interaction: yes, three asset cards arrive in order.
Audio intent: light placement sounds, one completion cue near 15.85s.
Audio-coupled idea: kit ready state.
Music: recedes as the message lands.
Transition mood: calm fade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Make your move — 3s
Return to navy. Job Hunter mark and “Focus your search. Make your move.” hold clearly through the end.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: warm closing note, bed fade.
Audio-coupled idea: logo arrival.
Music: fades out.
Transition mood: final hold.

**Music mood for this video:** steady and clean.
**Audio summary:** a restrained bed supports real interface actions, with one gentle payoff at the prepared kit.
